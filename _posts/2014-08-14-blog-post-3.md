hello, and welcome aboard my little website. this one has been years in the making. there have been multiple iterations of it in the past that have later been scrapped, but here i am: back to everyone's trusty [academic-pages](academicpages.github.io). also because i just graduated, finished my grad school interviews (never again - or maybe?) and for the first time in my life i have some free time without the threat of my to-do list hanging over my head. 

but first. a little introduction:

hi, my name is maitreyee. she/her, 22, gemini sun, elder sister. i have a bachelors in computer science and engineering (2026). i have always been drawn to CS for its interdisciplinary potential, and i have realised that i am most drawn to computationally representing real-world processes that are complex and chaotic on the surface. i enjoy connecting the things i learn about across disciplines!

my (academic) research interests sit at the intersection of deep learning, modelling and atmospheric and environmental dynamics, with a touch of human-computer interaction (HCI) because of my particular interest in underrepresented regional contexts. for my bachelor’s thesis, i spent a few months as a research intern at the Department of AI, IIT Kharagpur with Dr Adway Mitra on evaluating foundation model embeddings against satellite and ground observational data in cross-region crop phenology mapping. 

i am starting my masters in atmospheric and oceanic sciences at IIT Delhi very soon, which i am incredibly excited about. 15 year old me was a bit of a jack of all trades (even now, let's be real) chose CS and math as her favourite subjects because she thought they could set her up for studying just about anything in this world. i think she would be happy too.

(i'm also terrified though, but i think i have been long due for a very drastic life-changing "un-safe" decision.) 

i do also have a lot of questions and thoughts that i would love to turn into small, structured research projects as a hobby when time allows, especially about urban design, language, social sciences, anthropology and neuroscience. that would be way too many master's degrees, so i have to stick to my favourite one. lol.

a fun memory i often recount is watching shah rukh khan play a NASA scientist in _Swades_ (2004), also the movie which led me to realise that hey, i can study cs and still study weather dynamics. peak movie honestly, go watch it if you haven't! i really love 90s-00s Bollywood romcoms and dramas, as well as their soundtracks. i love consuming and creating art. my hobbies include (but are not limited to) drawing, fibre crafts, reading, baking/cooking, writing, playing musical instruments (uke/guitar/piano), and solving puzzles. this is not to say that i am good at any of them, i just think i could learn everything in the world a little bit. which may or may not be a bad thing. 

i want to keep updating this blog every now and then with my creative and academic pursuits - what i'm doing, what i'm learning, any exciting updates. as a way to document my life more than anything, but of course i'd love it if someone's feeling nosy and wants to join in! :D
