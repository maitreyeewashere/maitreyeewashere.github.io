hello, and welcome aboard my little website. this one has been years in the making. there has been iterations in the past that have later been scratched, but here i am: back to everyone's trusty [academic-pages](academicpages.github.io). also because i just graduated, finished my grad school interviews (never again - or maybe?) and for the first time in my life i have some free time without the threat of my to-do list hanging over my head. 

but first. a little introduction:

hi, my name is maitreyee. she/her, 22, gemini sun, elder sister. i have a bachelors in computer science and engineering (2026). i have always been drawn to CS for its interdisciplinary potential, and i have realised that i am most drawn to computationally representing real-world processes that are complex and chaotic on the surface. i enjoy connecting the things i learn about across disciplines!

my (academic) research interests sit at the intersection of deep learning, modelling and atmospheric and environmental dynamics, with a touch of human-computer interaction (HCI) because of my particular interest in underrepresented regional contexts. for my bachelor’s thesis, i spent a few months as a research intern at the Department of AI, IIT Kharagpur with Dr Adway Mitra on evaluating foundation model embeddings against satellite and ground observational data in cross-region crop phenology mapping. 

i am starting my masters in atmospheric and oceanic sciences at IIT Delhi very soon, which i am incredibly excited about. 15 year old me was a bit of a jack of all trades (even now, let's be real) chose CS and math as her favourite subjects because she thought they could be her trajectory to studying just about anything in this world. i think she would be happy too.

i do also have a lot of questions and thoughts that i would love to turn into small, structured research projects as a hobby when time allows, especially about urban design, language, social sciences, anthropology and neuroscience. that would be way too many master's degrees, so i have to stick to my favourite one. lol.
